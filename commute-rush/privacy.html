English

The short version. Commute Rush - Homo-tus works without an account. Your levels, stars, coins, village and settings stay on your device. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, your village progress is kept in the cloud under your account so it can sync with our other games and your devices. The app shows ads and records anonymous usage statistics, and you can turn those statistics off in Settings. Nothing is ever sold.

"We" and "us" in this policy mean this developer.

2. Data stored on your device only

The following never leaves your phone. It is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Level progress, stars and cracks for each level, and the level you are playing
  • Daily commute results
  • Coins and your village: buildings and residents
  • Settings (sound, vibration, language)
  • Reward-ad usage dates and play time used to space out full-screen ads
  • Whether you purchased "Remove ads"

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad ("watch an ad to double the coins of this block") and a full-screen ad when you move on to the next block, at most about once every ten minutes of play. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in advertising, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, device and OS information, and interactions with ads. We never receive your personal data from Google — only aggregate revenue reports.

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings. Purchasing "Remove ads" removes the full-screen ads; the optional rewarded ad stays available only if you choose to watch it. You can also limit ad personalisation from your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

4. Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics

To understand which levels are too hard and where people stop playing, the app records anonymous gameplay events — for example a level started or finished, the stars and number of bumps, a block of buildings completed, the daily commute played, an ad shown or declined, and a purchase completed. These events contain no name, e-mail address, phone number, or any information that identifies you personally. Firebase additionally collects standard technical information such as app version, device model, operating system, country and language.

You can switch this off in Settings → Privacy (share anonymous usage data). Collection stops immediately.

5. Homo-tus account and cloud sync — Google Firebase

The game works without an account: if you do not sign in, your village stays on your device and nothing is sent. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, your village grows as one across our other games (Sudoku Homo-tus, Block Mini, Last Rail) and your devices. For this the app uses Google Firebase Authentication, which receives an account identifier from Apple or Google (plus your e-mail address if you sign in with Google; we do not ask Apple for your name or e-mail), and stores your era, buildings and coins in Google Cloud Firestore under your account ID. Offline, nothing is sent.

Deleting your account: go to Settings → Homo-tus account and choose Delete account. Your account and cloud village are deleted right away; the village on this device stays until you uninstall the app. You can also ask us at rhdwpghl@gmail.com (send your account e-mail) and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Purchases

"Remove ads" is processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation from the store that the purchase succeeded.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area or the CCPA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to it. The app does not require an account and, unless you sign in, stores your game data only on your device, so you can exercise most of these rights directly:

  • Change your ad-consent choice and turn off analytics in Settings
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings
  • Delete your Homo-tus account in the app (section 5)
  • Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data

For anything else, write to rhdwpghl@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
