The short version. Walnut Eye Test works without an account. Your scores, daily results and settings stay on your device. The app shows ads and records anonymous usage statistics. Nothing is ever sold.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to Walnut Eye Test: Odd One Out ("the app"), a mobile game made by an individual developer under the lil_q Game Lab label, the same small studio behind The Walnut Shop eye tests on YouTube.

  • Google Play: developer hyeple, package com.lilq.eye_test
  • Apple App Store: seller SEOKGYUN IM, app ID 6838671091
  • Contact: rhdwpghl@gmail.com

"We" and "us" in this policy mean this developer.

2. Data stored on your device only

The following never leaves your phone. It is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Your best score and the results of each Daily Eye Test
  • Your daily streak
  • Settings (sound, vibration, language)
  • Dates and play time used to space out full-screen ads

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad ("watch an ad for extra seconds") and an occasional full-screen ad between rounds. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in advertising, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, device and OS information, and interactions with ads. We never receive your personal data from Google — only aggregate revenue reports.

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before requesting ads. On iPhone the app asks for permission before using the advertising identifier. You can also limit ad personalisation from your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

4. Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics

To understand which levels are too hard and where people stop playing, the app records anonymous gameplay events — for example a round started or finished, the score reached, a Daily Eye Test completed, a result shared, and an ad shown or declined. These events contain no name, e-mail address, phone number, or any information that identifies you personally. Firebase additionally collects standard technical information such as app version, device model, operating system, country and language.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area or the CCPA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to it. The app has no account and stores your game data only on your device, so you can exercise most of these rights directly: change your ad-consent choice, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data. For anything else, write to rhdwpghl@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
