Block Mini - Homo-tus

The block puzzle you know, with a civilization on top.

Every line you clear earns coins, and those coins grow a single campfire into a town, a skyline, and finally a city among the stars — 8 eras and 32 buildings, each with a short story from its time. Play Classic 8x8 with no timer, or a 45-second Speed round on a 4x4 board.
