English

The short version. Block Mini - Homo-tus has no accounts and no login. Your games and settings stay on your device; your civilization progress is also kept in an anonymous cloud record so it can sync with our other games. The app shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads and full-screen ads after a game ends. Nothing is ever sold.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to Block Mini - Homo-tus ("the app"), a mobile game made by an individual developer under the lil_q Game Lab label.

  • Google Play: developer hyeple, package com.lilq.block_mini
  • Apple App Store: seller SEOKGYUN IM, app ID 6816613436
  • Contact: rhdwpghl@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your device only

The following never leaves your phone. It is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Games in progress and best scores for each mode
  • Civilization progress in Homo-tus mode: coins, buildings, era, worlds
  • Settings (sound, vibration, language)
  • Daily bonus and reward-ad usage dates
  • Number of games finished (used to space out full-screen ads)

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows optional rewarded ads (for example "double your coins") and full-screen ads after a game ends, through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in advertising, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, device and OS information, and interactions with ads. We never receive your personal data from Google — only aggregate revenue reports.

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In the European Economic Area the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also limit ad personalisation from your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

4. Homo-tus account and cloud sync — Google Firebase (no analytics)

The app has no analytics SDK and no crash reporting tied to your identity. The game works without an account: if you do not sign in, your civilization stays on your device and nothing is sent. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, your village grows as one across our other games (Sudoku Homo-tus, Last Rail) and your devices. For this the app uses Google Firebase Authentication, which receives an account identifier from Apple or Google (plus your e-mail address if you sign in with Google; we do not ask Apple for your name or e-mail), and stores your era, buildings and coins in Google Cloud Firestore under your account ID. Offline, nothing is sent.

Copies of the app that were linked with a link code in an earlier version keep their record under a random ID, with an anonymous sign-in, until you sign in.

Deleting your account: in the village tap the cloud icon, or go to Settings → Homo-tus account, and choose Delete account. Your account and cloud civilization are deleted right away; the village on this device stays until you uninstall the app. You can also ask us at rhdwpghl@gmail.com (send your account e-mail, or the link code of an older record) and we will delete it within 30 days.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. The app does not require an account and, unless you sign in, stores your game data only on your device, so you can exercise most of these rights directly:

  • Change your ad-consent choice in Settings (EEA)
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings
  • Delete your Homo-tus account in the app (section 4)
  • Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data

For anything else, write to rhdwpghl@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
