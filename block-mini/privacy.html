Block Mini · Effective 26 September 2026

English

The short version. Block Mini has no accounts and no login. Your games, settings and civilization progress stay on your device. The app shows optional reward ads through Google AdMob. Nothing is ever sold.

1. Who we are

Block Mini ("the app") is an independently developed mobile game. For any question about this policy, write to rhdwpghl@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device only

The following never leaves your phone. It is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Games in progress and best scores for each mode
  • Civilization progress in Homo-tus mode: coins, buildings, era, worlds
  • Settings (sound, vibration, language)
  • Daily bonus and reward-ad usage dates

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows optional rewarded ads (for example "double your coins") through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in advertising, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, device and OS information, and interactions with ads. We never receive your personal data from Google — only aggregate revenue reports.

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In the European Economic Area the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also limit ad personalisation from your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

4. No analytics, no tracking by us

The app itself sends us nothing. There is no analytics SDK operated by the developer, no crash reporting tied to your identity, and no server of ours that receives your data.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Because the app holds no account and stores your game data only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly:

  • Change your ad-consent choice in Settings (EEA)
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings
  • Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data

For anything else, write to rhdwpghl@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

아래 정보는 기기를 떠나지 않습니다. 개발자를 포함한 누구에게도 전송되지 않으며 앱을 삭제하면 함께 사라집니다.

앱은 Google AdMob 을 통해 선택형 보상 광고(예: "코인 2배")를 표시합니다. Google 은 광고 게재·측정·부정 방지를 위해 광고 ID 같은 기기 식별자, IP 주소에서 추정한 대략적 위치, 기기 및 운영체제 정보, 광고 상호작용 기록을 수집·처리할 수 있습니다. 개발자는 Google 로부터 이용자의 개인정보를 전달받지 않으며 집계된 수익 보고서만 받습니다.

Google 의 데이터 이용 방식은 Google 서비스를 사용하는 사이트·앱에서 Google 이 정보를 사용하는 방식에서 확인할 수 있습니다.

유럽경제지역(EEA)에서는 광고 요청 전에 동의를 받으며, 설정 → 광고 개인정보 설정에서 언제든 선택을 바꿀 수 있습니다. 기기 설정에서 광고 개인화를 제한할 수도 있습니다.

  • Android: 설정 → Google → 광고 → 광고 ID 삭제
  • iOS: 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적

  • 설정에서 광고 동의 변경 (EEA)
