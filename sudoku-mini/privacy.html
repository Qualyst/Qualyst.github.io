English

The short version. Sudoku Mini has no accounts and no login. Your puzzles, settings and statistics stay on your device. The app shows ads and records anonymous usage statistics, and you can turn those statistics off in Settings. Nothing is ever sold.

1. Who we are

Sudoku Mini ("the app") is an independently developed mobile game. For any question about this policy, write to aimsamc25@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device only

The following never leaves your phone. It is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Your current puzzle and its progress
  • Settings (theme, language, vibration, highlighting, timer)
  • Statistics: games played and solved, best and average times, daily streak
  • Whether you purchased "Remove ads"

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in advertising, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, device and OS information, and interactions with ads. We never receive your personal data from Google — only aggregate revenue reports.

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ads are removed entirely if you purchase "Remove ads". You can also limit ad personalisation from your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

4. Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics

To understand which puzzles are too hard and where people stop playing, the app records anonymous events. These events contain no name, e-mail address, phone number, or any information that identifies you personally.

EventWhat it records
game_startedBoard size and difficulty
game_wonDifficulty, time taken, mistakes, hints used
game_abandonedDifficulty, time played, cells filled
game_failedDifficulty
ad_shown / ad_declinedAd type and where it appeared
daily_completedDaily-puzzle streak length
purchase_completedThat "Remove ads" was purchased

Firebase additionally collects standard technical information such as app version, device model, operating system, country and language.

You can switch this off. Open Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data and turn it off. Collection stops immediately.

5. Purchases

"Remove ads" is processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation from the store that the purchase succeeded.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area or the CCPA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to it. Because the app holds no account and stores your game data only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly:

  • Turn off analytics in Settings
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings
  • Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data

For anything else, write to aimsamc25@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

앱은 Google AdMob 을 통해 광고를 표시합니다. Google 은 광고 게재·측정·부정 방지를 위해 광고 ID 같은 기기 식별자, IP 주소에서 추정한 대략적 위치, 기기 및 운영체제 정보, 광고 상호작용 기록을 수집·처리할 수 있습니다. 개발자는 Google 로부터 이용자의 개인정보를 전달받지 않으며 집계된 수익 보고서만 받습니다.

Google 의 데이터 이용 방식은 Google 서비스를 사용하는 사이트나 앱에서 수집한 정보를 Google 이 이용하는 방법에 설명되어 있습니다.

"광고 제거"를 구매하면 광고가 완전히 사라집니다. 기기 설정에서 광고 개인화를 제한할 수도 있습니다.

  • Android: 설정 → Google → 광고 → 광고 ID 삭제
  • iOS: 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적

4. 사용 통계 — Google Firebase Analytics

어떤 난이도가 지나치게 어려운지, 사람들이 어디서 그만두는지 파악하기 위해 익명 이벤트를 기록합니다. 이 이벤트에는 이름, 이메일, 전화번호 등 개인을 식별할 수 있는 정보가 포함되지 않습니다.

이벤트기록 내용

끌 수 있습니다. 설정 → 개인정보 → 익명 사용 통계 공유 를 끄면 수집이 즉시 중단됩니다.
