Sudoku Mini

A clean sudoku. No ads while you play.

Sudoku Mini is built around one promise: ads never interrupt your puzzle. Interstitials appear only after you finish a board — and a single Remove Ads purchase removes them forever.

Features

  • 4x4 quick boards to classic 9x9, four difficulty levels
  • Daily Puzzle — one board a day, build your streak
  • Home screen widget, notes, undo, automatic note clean-up
  • Dark mode, 14 languages, works offline
  • No sign-up. Open and play.
