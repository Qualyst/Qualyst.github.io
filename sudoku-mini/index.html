Sudoku Mini

A clean sudoku. No ads while you play.

Sudoku Mini is built around one promise: ads never interrupt your puzzle. Interstitials appear only after you finish a board — at most once every 30 minutes. Every puzzle and feature is free.

Download on the App Store Google Play — in review, coming soon

Features

  • 4x4 quick boards to classic 9x9, four difficulty levels
  • Daily Puzzle — one board a day, build your streak on a calendar
  • Civilization — solve fast, earn coins, and grow a village from the Primitive Age to the Cosmic Age (8 eras, 32 buildings)
  • Post-game stats — beat your best time, see yourself improve
  • Home screen widget, notes, undo, auto candidate notes
  • Dark mode, 14 languages, works offline
  • No sign-up. Open and play.

Android: the closed beta is full — the Google Play release is in review and will appear here the moment it's live.
